# Pizza Sales Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive Excel dashboard that analyses pizza sales: overall KPIs, sales mix by category and size, order trends by month and weekday, and the best and least-selling pizzas. Slicers let you filter everything by pizza size, pizza category, and order date.

![Pizza Sales Dashboard](images/dashboard.jpg)

## What the dashboard shows

**KPI cards:** Total Sales, Total Customers, Total Orders, and Total Quantity.

**Charts:**

- **Total Sales per Category:** share of sales for Veggie, Classic, Supreme, and Chicken.
- **Sales Distribution by Pizza Size:** share of sales for S, M, L, and XL.
- **Monthly Order Volume Analysis:** orders per month, January to December.
- **Weekly Order Distribution:** orders by day of the week.
- **Top 5 Best-Selling Pizzas** and **Bottom 5 Least-Selling Pizzas.**

**Filters (slicers):** `pizza_size`, `pizza_category`, and an `order_date` timeline.

## A few observations from the dashboard

- Veggie is the largest category by sales (28%), followed by Classic (27%), Supreme (24%), and Chicken (21%).
- Large (L) pizzas make up the biggest share of sales (47%); XL is the smallest (3%).
- Friday is the busiest day for orders (91), and Wednesday is the quietest (48).
- The Classic Deluxe Pizza is the best seller, and the Brie Carre Pizza is the least-selling.

## How to use

1. Download the `.xlsx` file and open it in Microsoft Excel.
2. Go to the dashboard sheet.
3. Use the slicers on the right to filter by pizza size, category, and order date. All charts update together.
